## Content Guidelines

### Writing Standards

- **Voice & Tone**: Professional, approachable, and technically accurate
- **Audience**: Technical professionals, business decision-makers, and prospective clients
- **Style**: Clear, concise, and value-focused content
- **SEO**: Optimized for search engines while maintaining readability

### Content Types

- **Technical Articles**: In-depth technical insights and tutorials
- **Company Updates**: News, announcements, and milestone communications
- **Case Studies**: Client success stories and project showcases
- **Service Pages**: Detailed service descriptions and capabilities

## Review & Publishing

### Content Review Process

1. **Content Creation**: Author creates content following guidelines
2. **Technical Review**: Technical accuracy and implementation feasibility
3. **Editorial Review**: Grammar, style, and brand voice compliance
4. **SEO Review**: Search optimization and metadata validation
5. **Final Approval**: Leadership approval for publication
6. **Publishing**: Content goes live on website or blog

### Quality Standards

- **Accuracy**: All technical information must be verified and current
- **Clarity**: Content must be easily understood by the target audience
- **Value**: Each piece of content must provide clear value to readers
- **Brand Alignment**: All content must align with ThinkRED's brand voice and values

### Publishing Checklist

- [ ] Content follows style guidelines
- [ ] Technical accuracy verified
- [ ] SEO metadata complete
- [ ] Images optimized and properly attributed
- [ ] Links tested and functional
- [ ] Mobile responsiveness verified
- [ ] Accessibility standards met
- [ ] Legal compliance checked
