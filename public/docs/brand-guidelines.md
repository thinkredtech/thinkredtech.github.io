# ThinkRED Brand Guidelines

## Typography

### Fonts

- **Comfortaa**: Used for displays
- **Montserrat**: Used for headings and body text

### Display Text (Comfortaa)

- Display 1: 80/88, Letter spacing: -2, Medium 500
- Display 2: 48/56, Letter spacing: -1, Medium 500

### Headings (Montserrat)

- Heading 1: 32/40, Letter spacing: -0.5, Medium 500 / Regular 400
- Heading 2: 24/32, Letter spacing: -0.25, Medium 500 / Regular 400
- Heading 3: 18/26, Letter spacing: -0.25, Medium 500 / Regular 400

### Body Text (Montserrat)

- Body 1: 16/24, Letter spacing: 0, Medium 500 / Regular 400 / Semibold 600
- Body 2: 14/20, Letter spacing: 0, Medium 500

### Small Text (Montserrat)

- CTA Medium: 14, Letter spacing: 1.5, Semibold 600
- CTA Small: 12, Letter spacing: 1, Semibold 600
- Label 1: 14, Letter spacing: 1, Semibold 600
- Label 2: 12, Letter spacing: 1, Semibold 600
- Small Label: 10/14, Letter spacing: 0.25, Semibold 600
- Caption: 12/16, Letter spacing: 0.25, Semibold 600 / Medium 500
- Breadcrumb: 10/14, Letter spacing: 0.25, Medium 500

## Spacing

- 8px grid for finer spacing control
- 4px: Tiny (.25rem)
- 8px: Very Small (.5rem)
- 12px: Small (.75rem)
- 16px: Regular (1rem)
- 24px: Medium (1.5rem)
- 32px: Big (2rem)
- 40px: Very Big (2.5rem)
- 48px: Very Very Big (3rem)
- 56px: Huge (3.5rem)
- 64px: Giant (4rem)

## Shadows

- Regular: 0.8px 8px / 16%
- Small: 0.4px 8px / 16%
- Subtle: 0.4px 8px / 8%
- Subtle 2: 0.4px 8px / 8%

## Corners

- Default: 8px
- Big Elements: 16px
